# TrueFace3D evaluation protocol

**Chapter 1 scope, 21 November 2026.** Notebook 01 teaches one complete RealDenseFace case. Notebook 02 measures original inputs across all five baselines. Notebook 03 measures paired degradations across the same five and uses that evidence to choose any follow-up intervention. The [implementation plan](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) fixes the pilot metrics, subject aggregation, timing scope, and five-hour priorities. The Journal holds the measured results. The selection rules below apply to a subsequent frozen evaluation when enough labelled data and working runtimes are available.

## 1. How the papers grade a mesh

An RGB photo supplies 2D evidence. A matching 3D scan or multi-view stereo point cloud supplies the geometry reference. A model sees one photo per fit. Additional cameras, scan meshes and clean counterparts serve only as references, except for explicitly declared training and diagnostic tracks.

| Source | What the authors did | What we adopt |
|---|---|---|
| [DECA](https://files.is.tue.mpg.de/black/papers/SIGGRAPH21_DECA.pdf) and [NoW](https://now.is.tue.mpg.de/) | Align the prediction and reference using anatomical landmarks and the protocol's registration, then measure distance from reference scan points to the closest point on a predicted triangle. NoW grades neutral identity, even when the input is expressive. | Use the official neutral protocol. Evaluate the coarse DECA mesh in Chapter 1. Evaluate its detail separately in Chapter 3. |
| [MICA](https://zielon.github.io/assets/pdfs/papers/ECCV22/MICA.pdf) | Compare NoW with and without a fitted global scale. Metrical evaluation preserves scale error. | Report fixed-scale results only for outputs with verified physical units and use the official scale-aligned protocol in a separate column or table. |
| [Pixel3DMM §4.2](https://simongiebenhain.github.io/pixel3dmm/static/Pixel3DMM.pdf) | Use landmarks and ICP for rigid alignment, then remove hair, neck, ears and mouth interior from the reference. Report directional L1/L2 surface distances, normal cosine similarity and percentage within 2.5 mm. Its reference comes from calibrated multiview reconstruction. | Follow the reference-specific official equations. Use normal and distance-threshold scores where the reference supports them. Keep posed expression and neutral identity results separate. |
| [RealDenseFace §4.3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09238v1#S4) | Report NoW neutral results and NeRSemble SVFR posed and neutral L1/L2 Chamfer and normal similarity. Official SVFR test annotations are withheld. The paper reports runtime separately for known intrinsics and focal search. | Keep paper-reported scores separate from local measurements. Benchmark unknown-camera input under a consistent policy and show known-camera results separately if available. |
| [OpenDelight §7.1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05636#S7.SS1) | On FaceOLAT and rendered 3DRFE, reports PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS over a skin mask after per-image channel scale/bias alignment. Visual examples use unaligned predictions. | Show unadjusted error wherever color is calibrated. If reproducing the paper, report its color-aligned metrics in separate columns. |
| [TRUST / FAIR](https://trust.is.tue.mpg.de/challenge.html) | Uses uniformly lit neutral scans as approximate albedo. Reports ITA error per skin group and across groups, the spread across group errors and their composite score. | Use absolute ITA error on a compatible protocol. Report tone and local texture accuracy separately. |

## 2. Geometry metrics and units

For a scan point p and aligned predicted triangle mesh S, let d(p,S) = min over q in S of the Euclidean distance between p and q. If the nearest surface is 2 mm away, that point contributes 2 mm to the mean. For three distances 1, 2 and 6 mm, mean = 3 mm, median = 2 mm, and recall within 2.5 mm = 2/3. The nearest point can slide along a surface, so use regional checks and landmarks where available.

For an alignment with rotation R, translation t and optional uniform scale s, evaluate the transformed surface sRS + t. Set s = 1 after unit conversion for the metric track. A fit can match facial proportions after scaling and still predict the wrong physical size. The neutral track evaluates neutralized identity against a neutral scan. The posed track evaluates the expression of the actual input against its matched capture. Save the scorer's alignment transform with the error map, and keep the original estimation camera for overlays and UV baking. When rendering a heatmap on the reference, use identical numeric bounds and a labelled millimetre scale across methods.

| Metric | Interpretation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Mean scan-to-mesh (mm) ↓** | Average of d(p,S) over the defined face region. | Primary endpoint. Compute per-image metrics and aggregate by subject so extra views or augmentations do not overweight a person. On the main low-resolution endpoint compare paired errors on common successful cases and report coverage beside them. |
| **Median, p95 and region error (mm) ↓** | Typical distance, tail distance, and where the fit breaks. | Report per-face summaries and define regions from the reference annotation. Keep nose, cheek, mouth and eye-region scores only where the reference supports them. |
| **Normal cosine ↑ / angular error ↓** | Compare corresponding oriented surface directions with a dot product, or convert to angle in degrees. | Secondary shape diagnostic on reliable normals only, with surface mapping documented. |
| **Recall at fixed physical tolerance ↑** | Fraction of valid scan points whose distance is at most the tolerance. | Use the benchmark threshold (e.g. SVFR-related reporting where appropriate) and distinguish it from fit coverage. |
| **2D landmark reprojection ↓** | Project 3D landmarks into the original image and compare against independent 2D annotations, in pixels or with a declared normalization. | Checks camera/image alignment. Optimizer targets are useful diagnostics; agreement with them cannot establish 3D accuracy. |
| **Paired degradation penalty ↓** | Within model and subject, error(degraded) minus error(clean) against the same reference. | Shows how much quality was lost. Always show both absolute scores as well. For improvements use error(raw degraded) minus error(corrected degraded). |
| **Prior correspondence / relative-depth error ↓** | Original-image coordinate error and depth error in the declared scale, sign and joint-anchor convention on valid registered points. | Within-method diagnostics. When only a clean prediction is available, report drift, not ground-truth error. DECA does not emit comparable dense priors. |
| **Fit coverage ↑ and failure rate ↓** | Completed, valid fits or pipeline failures divided by attempted cases, broken down by condition. If there is an uncertainty threshold, show error versus accepted fraction and comparisons at matched coverage. | Counts no-face, invalid solver, invalid mesh, rejected fit and OOM separately. Report quality on the same successful cases where applicable. |
| **Compute** | Batch-one synchronized end-to-end latency, stage times, cold versus warmed, peak VRAM, parameters and FLOPs/MACs with operator coverage. | Same accelerator and precision policy across comparison. Separate model-only, detector/SR, solver and export. Mark unsupported custom operations as uncounted. |

Time batch-one reconstruction from a decoded image through a usable mesh, including detection, preprocessing, device transfers, camera search and solver. Record image decode and artifact export separately, and report complete CLI wall time as a second measure. Synchronize the accelerator before and after each timed interval. On the same runtime and case list, use five untimed warmups followed by at least 30 successful timed calls where feasible. Report the sample size, median, first-call latency with process/model loading described, and p95 only with adequate repeated timing (target at least 100 calls). Report failed attempts and time to failure alongside valid latency. A slower comparator that exceeds the allotted runtime receives its measured count and timing scope. Use allocated VRAM for the framework metric and flag external allocations it cannot see. Keep different devices and native precision trade-offs visible. RealDenseFace reports 64.4 ms for known intrinsics and 181.9 ms with focal search on RTX 4090. Those are author timings, useful for explaining why camera policy matters. [RealDenseFace §§4.3, 10](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09238#S10)

Never compare vertex index across different topologies. Use a common reference surface/protocol, face mask, alignment rule and units. Keep official L1/L2 definitions specific to the benchmark. Do not use image PSNR or LPIPS to rank geometry.

## 3. Skin appearance metrics

A skin **albedo** map represents diffuse surface color. A photo or captured texture may contain illumination, specular highlights, shadows and camera processing. A clean photo is a clean-input reference. A same-view original can score render fidelity only under matched pose/camera/light. True albedo accuracy needs a diffuse reference or a labeled proxy from controlled lighting/scans. Verify color space, exposure, camera/view, topology and UV registration before pixel comparisons. Preserve masks for observed, completed and excluded regions. Compare shared valid texels and report coverage separately.

| Metric | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Masked linear RGB MAE ↓** | Mean absolute channel difference against the registered diffuse reference, with fixed color calibration if available. | Primary intrinsic-color score on shared visible skin. Report reference provenance and separate observed/completed scores when labels exist. |
| **PSNR ↑** | Logarithmic pixel error score, computed as 10 log10(peak²/MSE). | Compatible paper comparison if color transform and range match. A blurry output may still score well. |
| **SSIM ↑ and LPIPS ↓** | SSIM measures local structural similarity; LPIPS measures distance between features of a pretrained network. | Secondary image comparisons with the same aligned region and fixed crop/mask protocol. Evaluate the same type of image or map across all methods. |
| **ITA error ↓** | Difference in a skin-tone angle calculated from CIELAB lightness and yellowness under the protocol. | Useful for tone on FAIR or compatible reference data. Cannot score freckles, placement or geometry. |
| **UV coverage ↑, seams ↓, held-out view/light error ↓** | How much skin is observed versus filled, how consistent shared UV boundaries are, and how the render matches an independent image when its camera/light are known. | Diagnoses texture projection and relighting. No claim about hidden skin without independent labels. |

For the primary appearance score, linearize both inputs according to the declared encoding. On a valid mask of N texels, sum the absolute RGB differences and divide by 3N. For example, a calibrated cheek texel with prediction (0.40, 0.25, 0.20) and reference (0.45, 0.25, 0.15) contributes (0.05 + 0 + 0.05) / 3 ≈ 0.033 on a unit scale. SSIM and LPIPS use neighborhoods, so hold their crop, valid-mask handling, image scaling and implementation fixed. Compute seams by sampling opposite sides of corresponding UV boundaries on the same mesh. Compare skin coverage by texel count in the declared skin mask and use the same mask/resolution in every row. Report matched-view render fidelity and diffuse color in separate result tables.

## 4. Data and selection

- **Local teaching:** existing source portrait and an exactly matched synthetic degradation. Display input, crop, maps, overlay, clay mesh, error/status and GLB. Treat a separately captured low-quality portrait as qualitative.
- **Geometry:** start with licensed NoW validation images and scans for neutral evaluation. Downloads require registration, and the official test scans are withheld. A locally partitioned NoW validation subset is a local study, with counts and identities reported as permitted by the license. Use licensed matched RGB, camera and scan from FaceScape for a posed pilot. RealDenseFace trained on FaceScape, NPHM and AVA-256, so dataset membership alone cannot prove independence. Audit identity overlap and qualify this comparison as in-domain or exposure-unknown where appropriate. NeRSemble SVFR stays an external benchmark. [NoW](https://now.is.tue.mpg.de/) · [FaceScape](https://nju-3dv.github.io/projects/FaceScape/) · [RealDenseFace §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09238#S4.SS1) · [NeRSemble SVFR](https://kaldir.vc.cit.tum.de/nersemble_benchmark/benchmark/svfr)
- **Appearance:** use independently qualified FaceOLAT or 3DRFE data where accessible, with matched source view, target encoding, diffuse reference, skin mask and licensed split. FAIR's reference supports the published approximate skin-tone task. Use registered views/textures from a capture dataset for projection diagnostics. Verify which type of reference it provides before making an accuracy claim. [OpenDelight §7.1.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05636#S7.SS1) · [FAIR](https://trust.is.tue.mpg.de/challenge.html)
- **Split:** lock development/holdout by identity before making any variants. Use one RGB view per run. Split multiple cameras, expressions and all degradations with the subject. Record checkpoint training provenance and treat unverifiable exposure as unknown.
- **Advance criteria:** require a valid editable deliverable, reproducible camera/units, agreed minimum coverage and runtime budget. On development, select the best primary low-resolution error while requiring clean non-inferiority and minimum coverage against the native control. If paired uncertainty leaves practical performance tied, use the faster/lower-memory choice. Freeze endpoint, minimum benefit, clean tolerance and budget before holdout. Report any tradeoff if no candidate wins. Do not tune the choice on holdout.

## 5. Freeze the decision before the final run

1. **Eligibility.** The geometry method must return finite nondegenerate geometry, verifiable model and camera conventions, and reproducible artifacts. The Chapter 2 base additionally needs validated topology/UV transfer, source-camera projection, edit/reset and reload. A lower score on neutral-only MICA does not qualify it for an expressive build by itself. Evaluate only comparable outputs for each track. Use RealDenseFace as the prior-correction host while reporting the best general baseline separately.
2. **Primary endpoint.** Select a main low-resolution condition on development before holdout. Start with a 64 px face-width candidate. Use subject-averaged, common-success paired change in mean scan-to-mesh error. Publish the absolute clean and degraded numbers plus paired intervals, failed/attempted counts and fixed-coverage comparisons. Keep neutral versus posed and metric versus scale-aligned protocols distinct.
3. **Promotion.** Before scoring, record the smallest useful scan-error gain, the clean non-inferiority margin, minimum coverage and the target-device time/memory budget. Start the development discussion with a planning floor of 95% valid fits at clean and at the primary 64 px condition, no increase in failures over the reference control for those conditions, and no OOM under the declared hardware budget. Review these proposed engineering gates against development evidence and freeze the values and decision rule before holdout. Measure score repeatability with identical reruns and registration sensitivity under the protocol. Estimate scan noise only where repeated reference captures support it. Rank eligible general-purpose candidates by the absolute degraded primary score. Select the strongest unlearned control for the residual adapter using development results. On holdout require the lower paired interval bound for its gain to clear the frozen practical margin and the upper bound for clean error increase to fit the clean tolerance. Resample complete subjects with their paired views/conditions when estimating intervals. With a small or unrepresentative sample, describe direction and uncertainty and keep the decision provisional. Report also the all-attempt outcome counts and a comparison at common coverage to expose selective failures. Within a predeclared practical tie, choose lower latency, then lower peak memory. A failed gate keeps the strongest verified baseline as the handoff.
4. **Skin decision.** Fix the same mesh, camera, source and renderer to select the appearance method. When testing geometry impact, swap only the geometry bundle under the same appearance method and source image. Keep the native camera for the end-to-end comparison, and add a controlled camera run if needed to isolate the mesh effect. Prefer lower error on shared valid diffuse texels, preserve coverage and clean tone, and inspect seams and new views. Use absolute color and per-image color-aligned scores in separate columns. Without a diffuse reference, compare matched appearance or synthetic known-target projection and label that result accordingly.
5. **Interpretation.** For example, if raw ViT-S scores 1.1 mm clean and 1.8 mm degraded, the penalty is +0.7 mm. If corrected ViT-S scores 1.5 mm on the same degraded cases, the gain is +0.3 mm. These are illustrative numbers. The clean reference image or teacher can be used to measure change and supervise training. Scan or verified independent targets establish accuracy.

### What to freeze and save

The frozen run manifest must identify the unit of analysis, intended identity split and allowed sources, benchmark region and alignment, scale policy, size/degradation bins, primary score, paired controls, all thresholds, target device/runtime budget and rules for failed or abstained fits. Save case-level rows, subject summaries and labelled plots. A comparison with unknown training overlap can still measure behavior of the fixed checkpoints on the declared benchmark. Independent generalization needs a verified split beyond known exposure.
